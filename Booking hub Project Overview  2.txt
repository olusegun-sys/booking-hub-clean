## 🎯 Project Vision & Mission

### What We're Building
**Booking Hub** is a multi-tenant booking management platform for Nigerian hospitality businesses. Think of it as **"Shopify for hospitality"** - not a marketplace like Booking.com, but a platform where each business gets their own branded booking page, accepts payments (Paystack or pay-at-venue), receives automated email notifications, and tracks all bookings in a real-time dashboard.

**Now part of Plazzaa:** The platform is being integrated into the larger Plazzaa ecosystem as the "Plazzaa for Business" solution - a comprehensive business management tool for hospitality businesses.

### Key Meeting Decisions (September-October 2026)
- **Iterate, don't rebuild**: Team decided to iterate on existing codebase rather than rebuilding from scratch
- **Independent B2B product**: Platform functions as standalone B2B booking tool, not tied to Plaza marketplace
- **V1 feature freeze**: Only customer booking page and business dashboard for V1
- **Pilot with 5-10 businesses**: Test with small cohort before wider launch
- **Feedback-driven development**: Only build features requested by businesses during sales outreach
- **Manual data migration**: Initial business data will be entered manually via Supabase
- **Launch deadline**: September 21, 2026 (extended)
- **Per-booking fee model**: Initial monetization via transaction-based service charge
- **Subscription tiers retained**: Free/Starter/Pro plans with booking limits

### The Problem We're Solving
Nigerian hospitality businesses (hotels, sports facilities, event venues) struggle with:
- ❌ No online presence or booking system
- ❌ Manual booking management (paper, WhatsApp, phone calls)
- ❌ No way to accept online payments
- ❌ No analytics or insights about their business
- ❌ Expensive custom development costs

### Our Solution
- ✅ Free branded booking page for each business
- ✅ Real-time booking management dashboard
- ✅ Paystack integration for online payments
- ✅ Automated email notifications for bookings
- ✅ Analytics and insights dashboard
- ✅ Mobile-first design (Nigeria is mobile-first!)
- ✅ Shareable booking links for WhatsApp and social media
- ✅ Database-driven content management (features, amenities, area guide)
- ✅ Multi-venue support - businesses can list multiple venues with individual details
- ✅ Owner properties page - View all properties from a single business owner
- ✅ Full property details - Ref ID, property type, bedrooms, bathrooms, parking, furnishing, listing status
- ✅ **Per-venue features, amenities, area guide, and property details** - Each venue has its own unique settings
- ✅ **Professional booking receipt** - Full receipt modal after booking confirmation
- ✅ **Venue-specific images** - Each venue has its own image gallery, separate from business branding

### Data Flow
1. **User** visits business booking page → selects room/dates → chooses payment method
2. **Frontend** sends booking request to backend API
3. **Backend** validates → creates booking in database → triggers email
4. **If Paystack**: redirects to payment → verifies on callback → confirms booking
5. **If Pay at Venue**: creates booking immediately → sends confirmation email
6. **Business Owner** sees booking in dashboard → can manage/update status

## 🚀 Complete Feature Implementation

### 1. Business Registration (3-Step Flow)
- **Step 1**: Account creation (email, password, full name)
- **Step 2**: Business details (name, type, state, city, address, phone)
- **Step 3**: Confirmation & approval pending
- **Ref ID generation** (BH-XXXXXXXX format)

### 2. Authentication System
- JWT tokens stored in localStorage
- Password hashing with bcryptjs (10 rounds)
- Role-based access (admin, owner, staff)

## 💡 Breakthroughs & Key Decisions

### Major Breakthroughs

1. **Indigo Branding Decision**: Chose Indigo (#4F46E5) as primary color for professional, trustworthy feel

2. **Mobile-First Design Philosophy**: "Design for worst-case scenario first (375px mobile), then expand for desktop"

3. **No JSX Decision**: Using `React.createElement` instead of JSX for more control and easier debugging

4. **Supabase for Database + Storage**: PostgreSQL familiarity, built-in storage, real-time capabilities

5. **Render for Deployment**: Simple deployment, free tier, Node.js support

6. **Unified Booking Page**: Single booking page for all business types - consistent UX, less code duplication

7. **Plazzaa Integration**: Booking Hub is now the "Plazzaa for Business" solution

8. **Subscription System**: Free/Starter/Pro tiers with booking limits and upgrade flow

9. **Database-Driven Content**: Features, amenities, area guide all stored in database - no hard-coding

10. **Nigerian Property Center Inspiration**: Premium UI design with glass-morphism, collage gallery, professional icons

11. **Lazy Supabase Initialization**: Fixed environment variable loading issues

12. **Static Icon Mapping**: Replaced `require()` with static `iconMap` for browser compatibility

13. **Multi-Venue Architecture**: Support for multiple venues per business with individual images, address, description, and pricing

14. **Venue Image Upload**: Desktop image upload for venues with Supabase storage integration

15. **Owner Properties Page**: Dedicated page showing all properties from a business owner with stats, filters, and listing cards

16. **Property Details Management**: Full CRUD for property details including Ref ID, Type, Bedrooms, Bathrooms, Parking, Size, Year Built, Furnishing, Listing Status

17. **Category-Based Features & Amenities**: Organized by categories (Interior, Exterior, Safety, Utilities, Outdoor) for better user experience

18. **ES Module Conversion**: Converted all components from CommonJS to ES modules for Vite compatibility

19. **🎯 Per-Venue Settings**: Moved features, amenities, area guide, and property details from business level to venue level - each venue now has its own unique settings

20. **Tabbed Room Management**: Redesigned RoomPage with 6 tabs (Basic, Details, Images, Features, Amenities, Area Guide) for cleaner UX

21. **Full Business Profile Editing**: All business fields are now editable in the Profile tab

22. **Call Modal Fix**: Simplified phone number display to always show business phone

23. **Booking Receipt**: Professional receipt modal after booking confirmation

24. **Simplified Booking Modal**: Single event date picker, removed guest count (capacity is fixed per venue)

25. **🆕 Venue Image Isolation**: Each venue now has its own image gallery - business profile handles only logo

26. **🆕 Auto-Bucket Creation**: Supabase storage bucket auto-creates on first upload - zero manual setup

27. **🆕 Booking Venue Selection**: Book Now button now uses the currently selected venue, not always the first one

28. **🆕 Clean Marketed by Card**: Removed unnecessary "View all properties" link since venues are already displayed on the page
